# 20250312 — Pricing Review

**Attendees:** Dana Whitfield, Marcus Ellery, You
**Related:** [[Areas/Pricing]] · [[MOCs/Pricing MOC]]

---

## Context

Quarterly pricing review. The main open item was whether to standardize contract terms across tiers ahead of H2 planning.

## Discussion Notes

### Contract terms

Dana opened with the cash flow case: annual contracts smooth revenue recognition and reduce the collections overhead that's been eating finance ops time. Roughly 40% of enterprise accounts are already on annual by preference.

Marcus pushed back on scope. His concern was mid-market specifically — those deals are more price-sensitive and the annual commitment is a bigger ask relative to deal size. He wasn't opposed to enterprise annual-only, just wary of extending it downmarket.

Discussed and dropped: moving *everyone* to quarterly. Billing overhead was the killer, plus revenue recognition complexity that finance didn't want to take on.

## Decisions Made

| Decision | Rationale | Owner |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| Annual-only for enterprise tier | Cash flow predictability through H2 | Dana |
| Mid-market and below stay flexible | Marcus's price-sensitivity concern | — |

**Explicitly conditional:** Dana said to revisit if churn moves. Nobody defined what "moves" means numerically, which is probably a gap.

## Key Insights

- The annual-only decision rests entirely on a cash flow argument. If the cash position changes, the rationale doesn't automatically survive.
- Marcus's mid-market concern was noted but not tested against data. No one pulled churn numbers by contract term.

## Open Questions

- What churn threshold would trigger a revisit? Undefined.
- What happens to existing quarterly enterprise contracts at renewal? Didn't come up.
