# Inbox — Unclear

Captures that `/process-inbox` couldn't route confidently.

Not a failure state and not a backlog to feel bad about. An item lands here
because the context needed to place it didn't exist yet — the meeting hadn't
happened, the Area wasn't created, the person had no note. Most of them route
themselves a week later.

Still fully readable by `/ask`. Nothing here is lost, it's just unfiled.

**SLA: 14 days.** Past that, `/brief` surfaces it and `/weekly-review` forces a
decision — route it, or delete it. An item nobody can interpret after three
weeks isn't being preserved, it's being avoided.

Drained by `/process-inbox unclear`.
